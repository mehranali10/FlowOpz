How much does it cost?

Our flat base rate is $10/hour with transparent tracking and zero lock-in. Need more capacity? A Weekly Sprint is $200 for 20 hours, and Agency Dedicated is $400 for 40 hours. No hidden fees or bloated retainers.

What is a GoHighLevel snapshot?

A GoHighLevel snapshot is a saved copy of a GHL account setup: pipelines, workflows, funnels, forms, calendars, custom fields and templates. Agencies load a snapshot into a new sub-account to launch a fully working CRM in minutes instead of rebuilding it for every client. We build industry-specific snapshots that are ready to deploy.

Can you fix or audit my existing GoHighLevel account?

Yes. Many projects start with a targeted fix or an audit call. We review your current GHL workflows, triggers, integrations and pipelines, find what is broken or slowing you down, and give you a clear proposal with scope, estimated hours and price before any work starts.

How long does a GoHighLevel setup take?

It depends on scope. Single webhook connections and workflow fixes take 2 to 4 hours. A multi-step automation or lead nurture sequence takes 5 to 10 hours. A full GHL sub-account setup with calendars and core pipelines typically takes 15 to 25 hours. You get a written estimate before any work starts.

What tools do you integrate with GoHighLevel?

We connect GoHighLevel with n8n, Make, Zapier, webhooks and REST APIs, plus tools like Stripe and Klaviyo. If an app has an API or webhook, we can usually connect it to your GHL account.

What happens when a project is finished?

It belongs to you. Every project ends with clean documentation, step-by-step SOPs and video walkthroughs, so your team keeps full ownership and confidence.
